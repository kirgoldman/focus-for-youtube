Features

Keep what is useful.
Cut the rest.

Every switch is separate, and pointing at one in the popup shows a picture of exactly what it hides.

Shorts, gone

Removed from feeds, search results, channel pages and the menu. Shorts links open in the regular player.

One toggleInstant

Feed and video page

Hide the home feed, topic chips and Mix playlists. On a video page hide:

  • recommendations and comments
  • description, like and share buttons
  • end cards, info cards, live chat

Interface

Hide the subscriptions list, the “Explore” and “More from YouTube” blocks or the whole sidebar. Voice search and promo banners go too. Autoplay is turned off.

Hide all, per block

Each block (Feed and Shorts, Video page, Interface) has a “Hide all” switch that turns the whole block on or off at once.

Presets and pause

Minimal, Study or Maximum in one click. Pause for 15 minutes or an hour, then everything switches back on by itself.

MinimalStudyMaximum

Less tempting thumbnails

Black-and-white covers stop the feed from shouting for your attention. The video itself keeps its colour.

Channels

You choose which
channels you see.

Block the ones you don’t want, or flip to an allowlist and see only the ones you pick. Handy for study and work.

Blocklist

Hide the channels you don’t want. A blocked channel’s page is covered and its video is paused.

Allowlist

See only the channels you pick. Shorts from other channels are hidden too.

Easy to fill

Add a channel by URL, @handle or name, with the “Add to list” button on any video or channel page, or paste a whole list. One click adds all your subscriptions. Search, copy or clear the list any time.

Privacy

Private by design.

Your settings and channel lists are saved in your browser’s extension storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Read the privacy policy

  • No account
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking
  • Minimal permissions: browser storage, access to youtube.com, and the active tab only while the popup is open
