Block the channels you don’t want, or flip it around and see only the ones you chose. Good for study and work.

Add a channel by link, @handle or name, with the “Add to list” button on any video or channel page, or paste a whole list. One click adds everything you subscribe to.

Nothing leaves your browser.

Your settings and channel lists are saved in the extension’s storage on your own device. There is no account, no analytics and no ads.

  • Only three permissions: storage, youtube.com, and the active tab while the popup is open
